Unlike traditional translators, I develop games myself. I understand narrative systems, player experience, UI limitations, platform requirements, and the realities of indie production – which means I do not simply translate words: I localise the experience.

My background spans literary fiction, technical documentation, academic writing, and interactive media. I bring the same rigour to a game’s UI strings as to a novel’s prose – because in both cases, the wrong word breaks the spell.

I work from English into Ukrainian and Russian, with a particular focus on game localisation, LQA, and narrative adaptation for interactive projects.

Why Work With Me
I build games myself
So I localise what I understand, not what I guess. I know how string tables work, what UI constraints feel like, and why a three-word button label matters.
Voice is everything
Your characters sound like themselves, not like translations. I preserve tone, register, and personality – because players notice when something feels off.
Full pipeline fluency
From Unity string tables to Steam store pages – I speak the full language of game production. No handholding required on the technical side.
One person, zero layers
You talk to the specialist, not a project manager. Direct communication means faster decisions, fewer misunderstandings, and better results.
