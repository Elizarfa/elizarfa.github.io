Attention to detail

I am a game developer and published author who has worked across the full creative pipeline — from writing and illustration to code and localisation. This rare combination means I do not simply translate words: I understand the mechanics, the narrative intent, and the player experience behind every line.

My background spans literary fiction, technical documentation, academic writing, and interactive media. I bring the same rigour to a game's UI strings as to a novel's prose — because in both cases, the wrong word breaks the spell.

I work between English, Ukrainian, and Russian, with a particular focus on game localisation, LQA, and narrative adaptation for interactive projects.
